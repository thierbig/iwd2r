- **The walk gate** (0x75CD0F-0x75CD40): more than three search squares away
  (`dx*dx + dy*dy > 9`, 16×12 cells), the action's destination is set to the
  target's `GetPos()` and `MoveToPoint(pt, 0)` answers for this call.
- **The roll** (0x75CD50-0x75CDBA): `GetAbilityScoreModifier(m_derivedStats.m_nSTR)`
  plus `rand() % 20 + 1`; the lock's difficulty is `m_lockDifficulty / 6 + 9`
  (the exe's unsigned `/6` magic 0xAAAAAAAB and `+9`), and a lock at 100 is
  unpickable. Success is `difficulty < roll + modifier`.
- **The door** (0x75CD84-0x75D12E): locked is `m_dwFlags & 0x2`; unlocked
  already answers `FEEDBACK_BASHDOORSUCCEEDED` (24). On success: a
  `CAITrigger(UNLOCKED, m_typeAI, 0)` + `CMessageSetTrigger`, the flags become
  `(flags & ~0x2) | 0x10`, `FEEDBACK_BASHDOORSUCCEEDED`, a `CMessageUnlock(new
  flags)`, and — when the door is still closed (`flags & 0x1 == 0`) — the owner
  toggles it (`ToggleDoor(m_typeAI, FALSE)`) when there is no service provider
  or the local player owns the door, else the multiplayer client's
  toggle-owner message (vtable 0x848C58, the open state's inverse) — **still
  unported** (0x75CF8A-0x75CFD7, the same TODO `UseDoor` carries). Failure
  answers `FEEDBACK_BASHDOORFAILED` (25).
- **The container** (0x75D040-0x75D3EC): locked is `m_dwFlags & 0x1`; unlocked
  already answers `FEEDBACK_BASHCHESTSUCCEEDED` (26). On success: the same
  `UNLOCKED` trigger message + `CMessageUnlock(flags & ~0x1)` and
   `FEEDBACK_BASHCHESTSUCCEEDED`; on failure a `PickLockFailed` trigger
  (`0x60` in TRIGGER.IDS, no named constant yet) + `FEEDBACK_BASHCHESTFAILED`
  (27).
- **The roll feedback**: both branches end with
  `FEEDBACK_ROLL(83, roll, modifier, difficulty, 0x4FEC)` unless the lock is
  100.
- **Unit test**: `tests/test_sprite_actions.cpp` "sprite: BashDoor rolls against
