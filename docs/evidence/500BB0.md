## Notes

- 0x500BBD: the `pData != NULL` assert (original line 12705).
- 0x500C03 and 0x500C55: `CGameRemoteObjectArray::Find` (0x59B300).
- 0x500CA3: `cnt == dwSize` assert (original line 12771), where `dwSize` is the whole message including the 3-byte header; this is why the round-trip test passes `dwSize + SPEC_MSG_HEADER_LENGTH` to Unmarshal.
- 0x500CD0: the FALSE epilogue when the target pair cannot be resolved and is not the (0, INVALID_INDEX) sentinel.
