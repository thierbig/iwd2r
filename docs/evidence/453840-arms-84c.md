(0x407A, 0x407B, 0x407C) and 71f429ae (0x40DA). Register roles as in the
other arm batches: edi = this (mov edi, ecx at 0x453869) and ebx = 0

ends in a bare jmp 0x45ADB7 to the release tail. 0x40DA resolves through
the unfiltered sub_40CCA0(this, FALSE), a NULL cause answering FALSE

## Arm notes

- 0x4061 and 0x4079 share their resolution, type gate and inline release
  exactly (the 84a NumItems pair's layout again); they differ only in
  what the sprite and container answers feed -- membership for 0x4061,
  a count for 0x4079 -- and in the compare that ends 0x4079. The port
  keeps the two arms' own comments, no shared helper, per the house
  rule for byte-adjacent arms.
- The 0x407A/0x407B/0x407C trio is one body spelled three times, one arm
  apart in the listing (0x4570DA / 0x4571C5 / 0x4572B0): identical party
  walk and total, sete, setg and setl in turn (0x4571BE / 0x4572A6 /
  0x457391), each ending in a bare jmp 0x45ADB7. The port repeats the
  walk three times the way the 0x40B5 TotalItemCnt family does. The
  walk gives up at the first member it cannot share (early epilogue,
  nothing else tried, no partial total), and counts the caller's own
  string1 against every party member including hidden and dead ones --
  membership is GetCharacterId's, not a filter's.
- 0x4079's other-type path answers FALSE without the compare (xor esi,esi
  at 0x4570A1), not a zero count into setl -- NumItemsLT(anything,
  non-container, 1) is FALSE, not TRUE. The port spells that as a break
  before the compare.
- 0x40DA's cause resolution is the unfiltered sub_40CCA0, so a NOONE
  cause resolves to nothing and answers FALSE through the release tail
  -- unlike the item pair's sub_40CB20, whose NOONE leaves through the
  early epilogue with nothing shared. Both spell FALSE; the difference
  is only whether a share would need releasing.
- 0x40DA's band is closed at both ends and order-insensitive: the exe
  takes max then min of the two bounds (0x45EAC0 / 0x45EA80), answers
  FALSE above the max (jg 0x45A887) and TRUE from the min up
  (0x45AD47). The static and sprite frame sources are both sign-
  extended from SHORT/WORD, so a frame above 32767 reads negative.
