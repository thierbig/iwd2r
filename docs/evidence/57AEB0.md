# 0x57AEB0 IcewindCProjectileScorcher::IcewindCProjectileScorcher

Imported in batch i058.

## Decompiled

The port is anchored on the exe bytes (see exemption). The ctor
chains `CResRef("AScorcT")` into the base `CProjectileSingleTarget`
ctor (0x578110), then tunes the wave: three-tick strike cadence,
60-tick clear cadence, no glow, a `TRA_M04` fire sound, and a speed
derived from the sprite's frame height (`cy * 3 / 4` — the
`lea (%eax,%eax,2)` / `sar $0x2` pair, signed round-toward-zero
division with the low half stored). It ends by zeroing the
segment bookkeeping, setting the 110-tick life span, requesting
height, and flipping `field_1C2` 0 → 1 (both stores are in the
exe: 0x57AFE4 and 0x57B062).

## Notes

- `m_speed` stores only the low 16 bits (`mov %ax,0x70(%esi)`),
  and the division is the signed round-toward-zero `/4` idiom —
  the port's `static_cast<SHORT>` around a signed `LONG`
  division reproduces both.
- `field_1C2` is written 0 (0x57AFE4) and later 1 (0x57B062);
  both stores are kept in the port in exe order even though the
  first is dead — the exe does the same, and the second store is
  what AIUpdate/Render observe.
- `m_callBackProjectile` and `field_182` both take the dword at
  0x851878 (`INVALID_INDEX`, -1) — the port spells both as -1.
