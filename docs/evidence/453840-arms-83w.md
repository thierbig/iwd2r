# 0x453840 CGameAIBase::EvaluateStatusTrigger, Task 83w arms

The dispatcher itself is 453840.md; this file records the eight small arms
Task 83w ported, in commits d9461576 (0x4094, 0x40DB, 0x40DC, 0x40E9 --
merged into the case bodies 83t/83u already wrote for their trios) and
c9422b6b (0x402D, 0x40D4, 0x40D8, 0x40DE -- new cases before default).
Register roles as in the other arm batches: edi = this (mov edi, ecx at
0x453869) and ebx = 0 (NULL), esi = bResult, the decoded CAITrigger copy at
[esp+0x34], pSprite/pObject at [esp+0x10], pObject2 at [esp+0x18], bNoRelease
at [esp+0x28]. Four arms resolve the trigger sprite through the vtable+0xA4
call (0x402D, 0x4094, 0x40DB/0x40DC, 0x40E9) and leave through the early
epilogue 0x45AB7A when it comes back NULL; three arms instead resolve their
cause through sub_40CB20 into pObject2 with a type gate (0x40D4 doors,
0x40D8/0x40DE sprites), whose null paths differ arm by arm as the notes
record.

## Arm notes

- 0x402D computes the percentage with the multiply idiom (lea x5, lea x5,
  shl 2 = x100) and jumps back into the setl tail of the arm listed
  before it; the port spells the compare out instead of reproducing the
  backward jump.
- 0x4094 shares its loads exactly with 83t's LevelGT/LT pair
  (0x4095/0x4096) and normalizes with the sub/neg/sbb/inc equality idiom;
  one case body picks the test by trigger ID, as the Morale trio does.
- 0x40DB/0x40DC share their loads with 83u's HPLostLT (0x40DD) and differ
  only in the normalizer (equality idiom, signed setg); one case body,
  same trio convention.
- 0x40E9 likewise shares its loads with 83u's LevelInClassGT/LT
  (0x40EA/0x40EB), GetInt1 into GetClassLevel, and normalizes with the
  equality idiom; one case body for the trio.
- 0x40D4/0x40D8/0x40DE all resolve their cause through sub_40CB20, but
  their null paths differ and the port keeps each literal: CheckDoorFlags
  zeroes its result in-arm (0x45AB39) and falls to the release tail;
  IsRotation jumps straight to the release tail with bResult still zero;
  FallenPaladin leaves through the early epilogue (0x45AB7A, nothing
  released). CheckDoorFlags also answers its AND RAW (bit value, not
  0/1), like 83u's 0x407E.
- 0x40DE is the only arm of the batch that re-checks the resolved
  object's type itself (GetObjectType() == TYPE_SPRITE via the vtable at
  +4) after sub_40CB20 already gated on it; the AND of the two bit tests
  (class mask byte & 7, flags bit 0x200) is its whole answer.
