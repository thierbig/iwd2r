# 0x757B40 CGameSprite::Hide

Member of CGameSprite in the original's GameSprite.cpp, `__thiscall`
`SHORT Hide()`, 2,300 bytes (0x757B40-0x75842F).

Ported in three ranges, one commit each: 275f0087 (the d20 roll, the
forced gates, the nearest-enemy witness lookup, the two skills and the
LIGHTMOD/light stealth score), 6515d7db (the no-witness contest and the
enemy hearing and sight contests), d834c236 (the still-hidden renewal
with its self-invisibility effect and the detected and forced reveal
tails). The caller arms are 2e18fc34 (case 18 in
CGameSprite::ExecuteAction and the CheckModal stealth cadence).

## Callers

Exactly two, both ported in 2e18fc34: the case 18 arm of
CGameSprite::ExecuteAction (0x72956F-0x72958D), which calls Hide only
when the sprite is not already hiding and returns its result directly
through the shared done label, and the modal case 2 arm of
CGameSprite::CheckModal (0x730EA2-0x730EC4), which re-runs Hide on
every third modal count (`m_modalCounter % 3 == 0`) so staying hidden
keeps being re-checked while the stealth modal state holds.

## Callees

No new callees: every call is in-tree — rand (the compat layer's
iwd2r_rand), GetDerivedStats, CGameArea::GetNearest, the 0x71F480
light sampler ported as CGameSprite::GetLightLevel, the sub_763E40
skill-base helper, GetRaceString, C2DArray::GetAt with atol on the
rule-table cells, FeedBack, CGameObjectArray GetShare/ReleaseShare,
GetAbilityScoreModifier, PlaySound, CGameEffect::ClearItemEffect and
DecodeEffect, CMessageAddEffect, CMessageHandler::AddMessage,
SetModalState, CInfGame::GetCharacterPortraitNum and
CInfButtonArray::ResetState. Average (0x5467E0) is inlined with a
note.

## Notes

- One rand draw decides the attempt: `nRoll = (rand() % 20) * 5 + 5`,
  and every outcome -- success, detection, forced reveal -- returns
  ACTION_DONE. The action is one try; CheckModal re-rolls it every
  third modal count.
- Two forced gates short-circuit before any contest: a natural 100 or
  a disabled stealth button (m_disabledButtons[0]) reveals. The
  forced reveal clears m_bHiding but leaves m_nModalState at 3 so the
  SetModalState(0) call runs the leaving arm of the stealth state
  (0x758339); the detected reveal instead clears m_nModalState first,
  which makes its SetModalState a no-op through the unchanged-state
  early return (0x7581F5). Both post HIDEFAILED (17120), set the
  stealth greyout to 90 and the selected button to 100, and run the
  toolbar-state guard chain.
- The stealth score is
  `((avg - light * LIGHTMOD * avg / 10000) + 10) * 5` with avg the
  mean of Hide and Move Silently and light the signed-char area light
  at the sprite (GetLightLevel, the 0x71F480 port); it is not
  clamped.
- No witness in range: a strict `nStealth > nRoll` decides (a tie
  reveals). With a witness, the hearing contest
  `(QUIETMOD + WIS mod + level) * 5` (floored at 0) against Move
  Silently is skipped when the enemy is deafened, the sight contest
  `(HIDEMOD + WIS mod + level) * 5` against Hide is skipped when the
  enemy is blind, and an enemy that is both falls back to the plain
  stealth contest. Each posts its FEEDBACK_ROLL line (0x9981 heard,
  0x9982 seen, 0x9983 unheard, 0x9984 seen-plain).
- Success plays ACT_07 only on the cycle the sprite first hides,
  posts HIDESUCCEEDED (19944), and renews a 300-tick self-invisibility
  through ClearItemEffect(CGAMEEFFECT_INVISIBLE), dwFlags 0,
  effectAmount 1, durationType 0x1000, duration gameTime + 300,
  DecodeEffect and a CMessageAddEffect to itself, then sets m_bHiding
  and m_nModalState = 3.
