# 0x75AAC0 CAIAction::CAIAction(SHORT actionID, LONG specificID)

`__thiscall`, `ret 8`, 489 bytes (0x75AAC0-0x75ACA6), emitted out of
line in the original's GameSprite.cpp right after Berserk. It builds the
three CAIObjectType members and the two strings, creates and destroys an
unused default CAIAction like the other constructors, then stores
m_actionID (+0x00) = actionID, m_specificID (+0xB6) = specificID, and
zero in m_specificID2 (+0xBA), m_specificID3 (+0xBE) and
m_internalFlags (+0xD2). m_dest is not written.

## Callers

is in sight.
