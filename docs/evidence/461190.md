## Notes

New port (`CGameAIBase::ForceSpell`, ForceSpell 113 and ReallyForceSpell
181; Ghidra's name is a B3AUTO placeholder). From the decompile, checked
against the disassembly:

- A dead caster (m_typeAI.m_nGeneral == CAIObjectType::G_DEAD, the BYTE at
  0x847C48) is done at once; no target is an error.
- The spell: the action's string, with the action's specificID (as a
  short) as the caster level; or, without a string, DecodeSpell of the
  specificID (an empty name is an error) with a creature's
  m_derivedStats.m_nLevel (+0x966) or 1. Levels below 2 are 1.
- `new CSpell(CResRef(name))` (the inlined CResHelper construction),
  Demand; unless the action is ReallyForceSpell, CheckAppropriateTarget
  (0x45DED0) must pass. The ability is the last whose minimum caster level
  (+0x10) the level reaches (the loop calls GetAbility twice per step, as
  FireSpell does). No ability is an error.
- The projectile (DecodeProjectile(missile type, this, 0)) is built before
  the casting; a creature's sub_727720 gives its spell level and its source
  is the spell.
- A creature (not for ReallyForceSpell) casts first: on the action's first
  tick (m_actionCount 0) sub_755A70 (the voice, sound and glow) at the
  target, a FORCEVISIBLE effect (136) when it is not visible
  (IsVisibleToCaller(FALSE)) and the target is not itself, a
  DISPELSANCTUARY effect (160) under sanctuary (derived spell state 20);
  both with duration type 1 (the WORD at 0x8491AA) at (-1, -1). The casting
  time is speed * 100 / 10 ticks: before the last 4 the conjure sequence
  (SEQ_CONJURE, 0x85BBB3) and ACTION_NORMAL; in the last 4 the cast
  sequence (SEQ_CAST, 0x85BBB2) with ApplyCastingEffectPost and
  ACTION_NORMAL; after it the cast sequence once more (when not yet set,
  with ApplyCastingEffectPost) and the spell goes off. The early returns
  delete the spell and the projectile.
- Each effect of the ability (CSpell 0x54A510 with the caster level) gets
  the caster's position and id, the target's position and the caster level
  (+0xC4); by target type: 1 self (flag 2, CMessageAddEffect), 2 the
  projectile, 3 the party (ApplyEffectToParty, then deleted), 4-8 the
  area's creatures through CGameArea 0x46E130 (TODO: not ported, in
  vis-probe's file; ours deletes the effect), others assert (line 1594).
- A creature's feedback 5 (FEEDBACK_SPELL) with the spell's name; a
  CMessageFireProjectile to the target; for projectile 304 one rand() %
  1000000 into the message (and, TODO, the projectile's +0x356); the
  projectile fires (vtable +0x6C) with 30 and 0; ACTION_DONE.

Rand: none of its own but the 304 projectile's; sub_755A70 draws for a
cast time of 3 or more. tests/test_combat.cpp pins 0 draws per tick for
Magic Missile.
