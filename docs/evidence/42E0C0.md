## Notes

The host's handler of PLAYERCHAR/85 "send me the character in this slot". It
needs an open session (`GetSessionOpen`, 0x8CF6D8 + 0x1032); the slot is the
SHORT at `pMessage + CNetwork::SPEC_MSG_HEADER_LENGTH` (the DWORD 3 at
0x85E670), `bSendCharInfo` is whether `GetCharacterSlot(slot)` names a
character (not `CGameObjectArray::INVALID_INDEX`, 0x851878), the requester's
name comes from `GetPlayerName(nMsgFrom, ...)`, and the reply helper is
called with all three. Always TRUE after the helper.

The original also constructs and destroys an unused `CSound` local here (the
same dead local `DemandCharacterSlotReply` has, 0x7A8BB0/0x7A8E20); it has no
effect and is omitted, as the sibling's port already does.

`tests/test_ui_misc.cpp` ("msg: the demand-slot update answers a closed
session and drives the reply", `[uimisc][msg][data]`) runs it on the engine:
closed session FALSE; the party's slot 0 (the full marshal path) and a slot
without a character (the header-only reply) both TRUE. The null provider
stops the send, so the wire bytes are read-only evidence. **It bites**: with
the two stubs 2 of its 4 assertions fail.
