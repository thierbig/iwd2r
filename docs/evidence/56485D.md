## Layout

`this+0x04 = this+0x08 = this+0x0C = controlled sprite id`,
`this+0x10 = controller id`. That is
`CMessage95(controlledId, controllerId, controlledId, controlledId)`:
the first link and both CMessage ids are the dominated sprite, the second
link is the controller. The 0x14-byte allocation and the vtable 0x84D37C
store agree with 0x4F6B80 and the factory's `operator_new(0x14)`.
