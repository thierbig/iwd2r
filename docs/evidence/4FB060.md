(dwSize includes the 3-byte header). The resref copy is bounded to 8 bytes
in the port (P4-10); the original copies the wire byte unbounded into its
9-byte buffer.
## Wire format

(PLAYER_ID m_remotePlayerID, LONG m_remoteObjectID; (0, 0xFFFFFFFF) when the
id does not resolve), then:

- +0x08 SHORT m_slotNum, +0x0A BYTE m_bCompressContainer, +0x0B the resref
  length and the resref bytes from +0x0C, then three SHORT usage counts
  (GetUsageCount 0..2), the SHORT m_wear and the DWORD m_flags. Total
  0x18 + resref length. The unmarshal reconstructs the CItem from the resref

  resolve.
