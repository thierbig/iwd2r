## Notes

Ported as CProjectile84E030::Fire. It takes the target's position and its source's (into m_pos), each with GetShare retried while SHARED or DENIED; on any other failure it returns without deleting anything (0x530CEC, 0x530D7B). dx = target.x - pos.x, dy = target.y * 4 / 3 - pos.y * 4 / 3; distance = (LONG)(sqrt(dx * dx + dy * dy) + 0.5) (the double at 0x848DD0 is 0.5); the velocity (vx, vy) = ((dx << 10) * m_speed / distance, (dy << 10) * m_speed / distance) (the WORD at 0x85EB66 is CParticle::RESOLUTION_INC, 10), or (1, 1) for a distance of 0; its length = (LONG)sqrt(vx * vx + vy * vy). It walks m_projectiles two at a time; for each projectile: a copy of each of this projectile's effects with the effect's projectile type (+0x9C) set to the projectile's, added to its list; its m_speed += rand() % 20 - 10 (one rand per projectile); m_nSpellLevel and m_sourceRes copied; Fire(pArea, source, target, targetPos, 0, nType). A pair, with k counting the pairs from 1 (a BYTE): the first drifts by (k * vy, -k * vx), the second by (-k * vy, k * vx), both with m_driftDecay = k * length / 40 and m_bTrackTargetHeight (+0xC4) = 1 unless the drift is (0, 0). A projectile left over (an odd count) gets no drift, m_driftDecay = this projectile's m_speed and +0xC4 = 0. Then m_projectiles.RemoveAll() and delete this. Tested by tests/test_projectile.cpp ("Magic Missile fires its missiles ...").
