# 0x71CEA0 CGameSprite::sub_71CEA0

329 bytes, `CGameSprite_B3AUTO_0071cea0` in the Ghidra names, address-named
`sub_71CEA0` in ours. No fork body (not a parked row's refused body; this is
an in-file dependency of 0x71CC90, measured and ported per the callee budget).

## Identification

Adds one weapon's specialization damage bonus to the derived damage bonus
for one hand: demands the item, resolves its launcher and takes the higher
proficiency of the two, looks up `wspec.2da` column 1 (damage; the
attacks-column twin 0x73CF10 reads column 0) at that proficiency row, and
adds the parsed number to `m_DamageBonusRight` (right hand) or
`m_DamageBonusLeft` (left hand). Empty slot or NULL ability adds 0.

- Callers: only 0x71CC90, at 0x71CD40 (`(pWeapon, 0)`) and 0x71CD4A
  (`(pOffhand, 1)`). `ret 0x8` = two stack arguments, both thiscall.
- House twin: `sub_73CF10` (src/CGameSprite.cpp:20654) shares the
  GetLauncher/Demand/prof-max/Release idiom and the
  `atol(GetAt(CPoint(col, nProficiency)))` finish, but 0x71CEA0 differs in
  three ways: no UTIL_ASSERTs, no character-portrait -4 arm, and the column
  is 1 (a .rdata WORD at 0x84EBB8, read `movsx` at 0x71CF58), not 0.

## Decode notes

- Prologue `sub esp,0x8` reserves one DWORD local pair; the SHORT `nBonus`
  lives at [E-4]: init 0 at 0x71CEB9, atol result stored at 0x71CFA1 (stack
  offsets differ because `push esi` at 0x71CEC7 sits between them), read
  back `movsx` at 0x71CFB9 after `pop esi`. All three refer to the same
  SHORT.
- `GetAbility` NULL at 0x71CEE0 jumps to 0x71CFA9 (the Release), skipping
  the launcher/proficiency/lookup block with nBonus still 0.
- `GetLauncher(pAbility, launcherSlot)`: the SHORT& out is `[esp+0x12]` at
  0x71CEE8 (the upper word of the first local); its value is never read
  here, same as the twin.
- `pLauncher->GetAbility(0)` result is discarded (0x71CF06) -- ported
  verbatim like the twin.
- The GetAt at 0x71CF58-0x71CF96 is the inlined C2DArray::GetAt (bounds
  checks against m_nSizeX/m_nSizeY at [game+0xBC4]/[game+0xBC6], m_pArray
  at [game+0xBBC], m_default at [game+0xBC0]), then cdecl
  `ToNumber` 0x7E7B03 on the CString's char data -- the same CRT atoi
  wrapper the twin's port spells `atol`.
- game pointer: `[0x8CF6DC]+0x1C54` fetched at the top (0x71CEAA) and only
  used by the range-2 lookup; the port fetches it at its use.

## Tests

CheckStatsChange; the Catch2 case in tests/test_check_stats_change.cpp

finished-character probe. Recorded with the 0x71CC90 test commit.
