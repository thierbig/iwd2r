# 0x707FB0 CGameSprite::SetTargetFly

## Disassembly reading

`__thiscall`, `ret 0xc`: BYTE circleType, BYTE facing and LONG radius at
`[esp+0x20]`, `[esp+0x24]` and `[esp+0x28]`. The body stores radius at
`+0x533C`, facing at `+0x533E`, clears `m_fCurrCircleChange` (`+0x5334`),
copies `m_pos` (`+0x06`/`+0x0A`) to `m_posOld` (`+0x5362`/`+0x5366`), clears
`m_turningAbout`/`m_walkBackwards`, and calls `SetSequence(10)` (0x707230) at
0x707FE0-0x708024.

- `FLY_0` (0 at 0x85C0A4): facing is cleared and `m_fCircleChange`
  (`+0x532C`) is `radius / GetMoveScale` (0x70803B-0x70808D).
- otherwise `m_fCircleChange` is
  `radius * circleType * pi / GetMoveScale() / 2`
  (0x7080C8-0x708139).
- the direction switch at 0x70813F-0x7081CD sign-extends `m_circleFacing`
  with `movsx` (0x70815E, 0x708178, 0x708192, 0x7081A9): direction 0 adds
  `radius * side` to `m_posOld.x`, 4 adds `3 * radius * side / 4` to y, 8
  subtracts `radius * side` from x, and 12 subtracts
  `3 * radius * side / 4` from y.
- `m_fDirectionOffset` (`+0x5340`) is `(8 - m_nDirection) * pi / 8`
  (0x7081E7-0x70822C); the comparison at 0x7081F9 sign-extends
  `m_circleFacing` and, when it equals `FLY_RIGHT` (-1 at 0x85C09C), adds pi
  (0x70823B-0x708245).

The sign extension is observable: `FLY_RIGHT` is passed as 0xFF (RandomFly
draws it at 0x7526D0), and an unsigned read would turn it into 255 for both
the initial hop and the `FLY_RIGHT` comparison.

## Notes

Ours keeps `m_circleFacing` at `+0x533E` as the original's BYTE for layout,
then sign-extends it once into a SHORT local for the four switch arms and the
`FLY_RIGHT` test. The data test sets up action 101 for both `FLY_LEFT` and
`FLY_RIGHT` at direction 0 and radius 512 and checks `m_posOld`
(1512,1000) versus (488,1000), the pi versus 2*pi direction offset, and one
`AIUpdateFly` tick whose x coordinates straddle the start point
symmetrically. It tests our engine only, not original-process parity.
