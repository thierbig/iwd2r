constructor takes no arguments, returns `this`, and:

## Notes

- The `movw $0x37,0x2a0(%esi)` store is the reason the class
  exists as more than a typedef: +0x2A0 is the WORD lifetime the
  SPBRNHND update path counts down (the fork names it
  `m_animationLifetime`). The BAM ctor 0x52AD60 never writes
  +0x2A0/+0x2A1 (its last member stores there are the byte at
  +0x29D and the WORD 0x7FFF at +0x29E), so the bytes belong to
  subclasses — `CProjectileBAM`'s `field_2A0`/`field_2A1` remain
  the base-class documentation of the same bytes, exactly like
  `CProjectile84E030`'s `m_nCount`.
- field_174/field_178 (23/50) have no known exe name; they are
  stored unconditionally by this ctor and read elsewhere in the
  SPBRNHND family (not in this batch). The port keeps them as
  bare fields on `CProjectile`.
- The palette call here is the full range (0, 71) — the whole
  72-entry palette off the master bitmap, unlike `CProjectileDagger`'s
  two partial ranges.
