# 0x71C0C0 CGameSprite::CheckStatsChange

3,021 bytes, `CGameSprite_CheckStatsChange` in the Ghidra names,
`sub_71C0C0` in ours. Parked as "called on one side only" in nine
scenarios (docs/wso/import-parked/71C0C0.json), so the original and ours
already diverge; no fork body exists.

## Identification

Recomputes derived state after a creature's stats change: refreshes each
spell list's slot maxima, reconciles the innate ability granted for high
charisma (SPIN970) with the charisma modifier, applies the
CON-hit-point bonus delta and ability-based saving-throw modifiers
(paladins add their charisma modifier to all three saves), then, for
sprites with a portrait, caps XP and checks whether the next level is
reached.

- Sole exe caller: 0x72F278.
- House sibling: the 0x71E760-adjacent loop at src/CGameSprite.cpp:8863
  shares the spell-maxima idiom but does NOT clamp field_18 -- the
  0x71C0C0 asm does (unsigned compare), and the asm wins here.
- The innate global the exe reads at 0x8F8E18 is the CRT dynamic
  initializer at 0x6EF610: `CResRef stru_8F8E18("SPIN970")`, already
  in-tree as `CGameSprite::SPIN970` (src/CGameSprite.cpp:551). The other
  exe consumer is 0x71E72B (AddSpecialAbility loop).

## Tests

`tests/test_check_stats_change.cpp` (commit 694a1774) drives the port on
the finished-character probe: the CON bonus delta (floor 1, the dead
sprite's untouched hit points, the increase arm), the slot-maxima clamp
of field_18, the three ability save modifiers plus paladin divine grace,
the XP cap and the m_bLevelUp flag against a GetNextLevel oracle. The
SPIN970 reconciliation arm is reached but not asserted: whether it does
anything depends on the game's innate-spell list containing SPIN970.

The parked scenarios (docs/wso/import-parked/71C0C0.json) call this on
one side only, so no existing scenario exercises it. The sole exe caller
is 0x72F278 inside CGameSprite::ProcessEffectList (0x72DE60), in the
range our tree still carries as `TODO: Incomplete (0x72E759-0x72FB24)`
-- nothing in our tree calls CheckStatsChange until that range lands.
