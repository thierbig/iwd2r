## Notes

New port (`CGameSprite::Swing`, IEex's CGameSprite_Swing). One frame of an
attack round: at the round's start (m_attackFrame -1) the speed (the
ability's speed less the physical speed, UtilRandInt(6, -luck) - 3, monk
levels 8 and 12 and improved initiative one each, clamped 0..10); the frame
type from the animation (0x73B6EC byte table, 0x73B6D0 jump table): 0 idle,
6 the swing starts, 7 and 8 between, 9 the blow (Hit, then Damage and the
ability's effects on a hit, the hit-by trigger, a charge spent), 15 the
round ends, the rest (10..14) pick the attack of the round.

Partial: the area-wide effect targets (CGameArea 0x46E130) are a TODO.
The charge a blow spends refreshes the weapon's quick buttons:
CheckQuickLists(&abilityId (item type 2, the slot, the ability), -1, 0, 0)
at 0x73B187-0x73B1B8, ported in the M1/M2 review fix round (item 3). Ours has one loop
per effect list where the original has two copies.

Combat stream, recovery session: the missile branch of the blow
(0x73A679-0x73AC06) is ported from the disassembly (Ghidra loses the
projectile's register and reuses stack slots). A local copy of

kept. The shoot sequence (SEQ_SHOOT, 0x85BBBA) when not in it and attacking
this frame. Without a current projectile (+0x5592) one is decoded from the
ability's missile type (+0x2A) and the weapon identification loaded (into
an unread local), as the original does; with none or no attack this frame
nothing more. Otherwise: Hit (the attack d20s); a miss clears the
projectile's effects (0x529F10). A hit marks the blow (the charge test at
