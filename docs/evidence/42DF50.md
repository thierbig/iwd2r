## Notes

The port is the new `CBaldurMessage::DemandCharacterSlotUpdate(SHORT, SHORT)` in
`src/CMessage.cpp`. It keeps the connection guard, the `GetPlayerName` +
empty-name guard, the 2-byte `{SHORT nCharacterSlot}` payload and the
`SEND_GUARANTEED` PLAYERCHAR 80/85 send to the named player, and answers TRUE
after the send.

The payload is the same SHORT slot used by the receive side
`OnUpdateDemandCharacterSlot` (0x42E0C0) and by `OnUpdateCharacterSlotReply`
(0x42E630), which the caller invokes after the wait.

Proof is the unit test
`CBaldurMessage::DemandCharacterSlotUpdate sends the update demand to a player`
in `tests/test_network.cpp` (`[msg][network]`, no `IWD2_DATA`): with the
connection established and player slot 1 visible and named, the call answers
TRUE and `CNetwork::STATIC_MESSAGE_BUFFER` contains `0xFF, 80, 85, 4, 0` (the
SHORT slot 4, little-endian) and nothing past it; an empty player name and a
closed session both answer FALSE without touching the buffer.
