# 0x5838A0 CLUAConsole::Execute

The console line dispatcher, `__thiscall` `void Execute(CString sInput)`,
1,364 bytes (0x5838A0-0x583DEE, `ret 4` at 0x583DF1; dispatch tables at
0x583E34 and 0x583DF4). Ghidra: CLUAConsole_Input.

## Line shape

`CtrlAltDelete:COMMAND(args)` — the prefix word left of the first colon,
upper-cased and run through the word encoder 0x5844E0, must equal the
encoded "CTRLALTDELETE" baked at 0x8AF630, or the line is refused with
"Nice try cheater!" (0x8AF700). No colon at all (or one at position 0) is
the same refusal. The check is on the upper-cased line, so the prefix is
case-insensitive; the command body is then re-derived from the
original-case parameter before its own MakeUpper, so the whole line is
case-insensitive end to end.

## Body

Empty input returns silently. After the prefix check: `Find('(')` on the
upper-cased tail; a miss or a leading paren stores field_0 = -1 and
reports CommandNotFound, then falls through — the parse still runs with
the clamped empty word (Left of a negative count is empty), so
ResolveCommand reports on the empty word too: a valid-prefix line
without '(' is reported twice. Otherwise ResolveCommand(sLine.Left(n))
maps the word to an id (0x583E60) and ParseArgs(sLine.Right(len-n-1))
files the arguments (0x5840E0); ParseArgs can leave field_0 = -2 on a
malformed list, and the dispatch gate (signed compare at 0x583A50)
rejects every negative id before dispatch.

Dispatch: field_0-12 above 0x1B unsigned is the -3 report (0x583DC5);
otherwise byte[0x583E34 + id-12] picks one of sixteen arms (jump table
0x583DF4). The table bytes: `0 1 2 15 3 4 5 15 15 6 15 15 7 8 9 15 10 11
15 15 15 15 15 15 15 12 13 14` — arm 15 is the dead -3 path (the range
check already handled every id whose byte is 15, and ResolveCommand
never yields those ids).

Value-table reads go straight through the array's m_pData in the listing
(0x583AAD, 0x583C93, 0x583B9E); text arguments go through the shared
element accessor 0x65F8F0 (11 bytes, `ret 4`: &m_pData[i]) with the token
copy-constructed into the handler argument (0x7FC98F). The use counts of
CREATEITEM are low-word truncations of the DWORD values (the fourth arm
reads [m_pData+8] directly). Arms 0-2 hand the handler this+0x40 instead
of the this+0x38 every other arm uses; no ported handler reads either.

## Callers

Not wired: the Lua console edit's Enter handler (0x77BF30) is not ported
(iwd2r has no Lua runtime); this port adds the callee, not the caller.

## Test

tests/test_cluaconsole.cpp [clua], Task 85d: the empty-line silent return
leaves field_0 untouched, and ENABLECHEATKEYS lines drive the full
pipeline (prefix check, resolve, parse, count, dispatch) through to the
world-screen flag over the zeroed chitin stub from
tests/test_ctrl_alt_delete.cpp — including the shape-table quirk that a
wrong count (two values) falls back to the no-argument arm and enables
the keys. Printing arms stay untested for the same reason as the parse
layer: they route through CScreenWorld::DisplayText into a live world
screen.
