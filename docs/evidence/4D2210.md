## Notes

The periodic pump, host only: while the retry counter (+0x28) is at most 5, a
gap of more than 3,750 ms since +0x02 resends the heartbeat and increments the
counter; a gap of more than 30 s resends it again and restamps +0x02. It then
takes the reply section, dequeues one datagram (0x4D0B80) and hands it to
0x4D15D0, freeing the datagram when its length is non-zero, and returns TRUE;
with the heartbeat off or when not the host it returns FALSE without touching
the socket.

The unit tests cover the early-outs and a tick where the heartbeat is due and
one query is queued: the heartbeat datagram, the reply datagram and the retry
counter are all pinned.
