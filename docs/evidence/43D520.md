## Notes

1. The sender check is the host's *player location*, not the host's PLAYER_ID: the exe compares
   `nMsgFrom` with `FindPlayerLocationByID(GetHostPlayerID(), FALSE)` (the same pattern as the
   other host-checked handlers in the file). The existing port compared `nMsgFrom` with
   `GetHostPlayerID()` directly; that is a location-versus-id mismatch and is fixed here.
2. The option is the BYTE at `SPEC_MSG_HEADER_LENGTH` of the payload; the guard is the client's
   (session open, not hosting).
