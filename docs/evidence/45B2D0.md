# 0x45B2D0 sub_45B2D0

A free static in the original's GameAIBase.cpp neighborhood,
`CPoint* sub_45B2D0(CPoint* pDest, LONG x, LONG y)`, 46 bytes
(0x45B2D0-0x45B2FD). Ported with the LOS arm that calls it (commit
0793bc4a), as a file-static in src/CGameAIBase.cpp.

## What it does

The world-to-grid conversion behind the LOS trigger's range test: both
coordinates are divided, signed (cdq/idiv at 0x45B2DC/0x45B2DE and
0x45B2EE/0x45B2EF), by the path-search grid's byte constants --
0x84D6A0 for x (CPathSearch::GRID_SQUARE_SIZEX) and 0x84D6A1 for y
(GRID_SQUARE_SIZEY) -- and the two quotients are stored through the
destination pointer ([eax] and [eax+4] at 0x45B2F7/0x45B2F9), which is
returned. The first division's quotient is parked in esi across the
second (push/pop esi 0x45B2DD/0x45B2FC).

## Callers

The CGameAIBase::EvaluateStatusTrigger (0x453840) LOS arm 0x4013, twice:
0x454672 converts the resolved target's position and 0x454697 this
object's own, both into stack CPoints that sub_45B300 then squares the
distance between.
