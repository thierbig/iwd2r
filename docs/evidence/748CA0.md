# 0x748CA0 CGameSprite::RandomWalkContinuous

Member of CGameSprite in the original's GameSprite.cpp, `__thiscall`
`SHORT RandomWalkContinuous(void)`, 2624 bytes (0x748CA0-0x7496E0).
Ported in commit ca96cf3.

## Body

The RandomWalkContinuous (200) action: a RandomWalk (0x747F80) that never
pauses. The prologue default-constructs the final CAIAction local in place
(two empty-string assigns, 0x748D67/0x748D78) and a CAIObjectType narrowed
from ANYONE by Set (0x748DB6). An interrupt returns ACTION_INTERRUPTABLE
([0x8485C6]) with nothing queued; only then is the copy of the action
itself built (actionID from m_curAction, specificID 0/-1/0, dest (-1,-1),
internalFlags 0) and queued at vt+0x74 (0x748F5D).

The walk box is the area viewport copied at 0x748F6E (CopyRect of
m_cInfinity.rViewPort), width and height each clamped to m_huntingRange
(WORD at 0x3E78) when twice the range is still smaller than the dimension.
RandomWalk's frame-rate and viewport gates are absent here.

The hunt: base-stat flag bits 24-30 (0x748FCA-0x74905F) copy the sprite's
own m_typeAI enemy-ally/general/race/class/specific/gender/alignment bytes
into the search type; any of the first six sets the filter flag (the ebx
that later doubles as GetNearest's checkLOS). The alignment arm
(0x40000000) copies its field and skips the flag store, but its jump at
0x749057 lands on the hunt itself (0x749065), past the flag test at
0x749059, so alignment alone arms the hunt too. Armed (0x749065), the nearest match
comes from CGameArea::GetNearest (0x46B160) over GetVisualRange/
GetVisibleTerrainTable/GetCanSeeInvisible - with no INVALID_INDEX check
before the share. The GetShare retry loop (0x7490A0-0x7490DE,
THREAD_ASYNCH) runs to SUCCESS/other; QUIRK: a non-SUCCESS return, or a
SUCCESS whose pointer is NULL (0x7490EA), skips everything including
ReleaseShare. On a live object the raw position deltas (m_pos at +6/+0xA)
are absoluted and capped at half the box, re-signed, and kept as the hunt
push (0x7490EC-0x749148); ReleaseShare follows at 0x749148.

The draw loop (0x74916B) is RandomWalk's: rand()%width - width/2 and
rand()%height - height/2 with the raw distance squared kept for the leash;
the leash base is m_pos while within m_huntingRange of m_posStart (linear
compare, 0x749210) and m_posStart otherwise; the hunt push is added before
the area-bound clamps (nAreaX/nAreaY); GetCost (0x547B10) runs on the grid
square with m_animation.GetPersonalSpace() (vt+0x5C; the original asserts
m_animation first at 0x7492B7, dropped for the embedded member), retrying
an impassable draw up to 100 times. A (-1,-1) m_posStart forces the
distance to -1 (always in leash). QUIRK: unlike RandomWalk there is no Wait
fallback anywhere - after 100 impassable draws or 20 leash rejections the
last point is accepted as-is, and the leash compare is linear while the
accept compare is range squared (0x749344), RandomWalk's asymmetry.

The tail builds the MoveToPoint in place (actionID 23 [0x8477B0],
specificID -2/-1, dest the drawn point) and member-wise copies it into the
prologue local (0x749548), queued at vt+0x74 (0x7495DF) before
ACTION_DONE ([0x8485C4]). Two dead default-constructed CAIAction
temporaries (0x748EF1, 0x7493FA) are compiler artifacts, omitted.

## Test

Driven through the wired dispatcher arm in tests/test_sprite_actions.cpp
[ai][data]: the interrupt answers ACTION_INTERRUPTABLE and queues nothing;
the frame-rate and viewport conditions that make RandomWalk queue a Wait
leave this body moving (always MoveToPoint with specificID -2 plus the
self-copy); a hunting range of 40 halves into a 40x40 box around the
sprite; the enemy-ally flag bit arms the hunt end to end and a GetNearest
miss is a bad index the walk shrugs off. Not asserted: the alignment-alone
hunt and the found-match push (not observable from the queue) and the
100/20 give-up tails (need a uniformly impassable bitmap).
