## Arm notes

- 0x401B finishes the Reputation family 83z opened (0x4019 Reputation /
  0x401A ReputationGT): identical party test and reputation loads, setl
  where they had sete and setg. The port lives in the same case body as
  the other two labels, picking the compare by trigger ID.
- 0x4077 and 0x4078 are the same arm spelled twice, one apart in the
  listing (0x456EAB / 0x456F54): identical cause resolution, type gate
  and count reads, equality against setg. The exe spells the whole walk
  twice, one arm per trigger; the port shares the body and picks the
  compare by trigger ID, the way the NumCreature trio does.
- 0x401F's byte truncation (and eax, 0xff at 0x455098 and the same
  instruction in each band slot, IsDawn's at 0x4550BD) is invisible to
  the port: the band predicates read m_gameTime modulo the day length,
  which the truncation cannot change. The unsigned gate is the port's
  static_cast<ULONG> check, negatives excluded rather than wrapped --
  the original's ja lands on 0x45AB39 (result zero plus release).
- 0x40AB reads the int1 through GetInt1, so the mode travels in the
  trigger's m_specific2 (+0x46); an int1 the dec/je chain does not reach
  (0, negative, or above 3) zeroes the result instead of defaulting to a
  compare -- the port's nMode >= 1 && nMode <= 3 gate.
- 0x40C6 is the batch's only arm that dereferences the caller's own area
  through the raw member (+0x12) with no null check: a caller outside an
  area crashes in the original, and the port reproduces the read as-is.
  The zero-specifics fallback takes the larger of the two area
  dimensions as the range (jle favoring nAreaY on a tie).
- 0x40CF tolerates every miss (no digit, null area, null cause) through
  the release tail with a zeroed result -- unlike 0x4077/0x4078, whose
  null cause takes the early epilogue. The in-place cause Decode is the
  0x400A Alignment pattern from 83x: Decode(m_triggerCause) rather than
  Decode(triggerCopy), so the rest of the trigger keeps its raw
  spec fields.
- 0x40DF chains two getters the listing never null-checks: GetAnimation
  returns the embedded CGameAnimation object itself (never NULL), and
  GetAnimationId's UTIL_ASSERT is the only guard on the inner
  m_animation pointer. The test installs a zeroed CGameAnimationType to
  pass it.
