# 0x52F260 CProjectileSkullTrap::CProjectileSkullTrap

The skull trap and glyph carrier (vtable 0x84DD80): Skull Trap rides this
with the sklT travel cell and ShSkull burst, Glyph of Warding with
glphwrdT and ShGlyph. It binds the spell's travel BAM through the
exploding base, makes the burst BAM a non-double-sized shadow cell,
doubles the native WORD speed, sets one strike of a 100-tick linger with
the pre-scan on, and arms both embedded explosion cells — cell 1 with the
travel BAM, cell 2 with the burst BAM, each flagged active only when its
ref is non-empty. The +0x4B4 member the decoder stamps with the burst
sound is a 4-byte VC6 CString, not a CResRef (see Notes).
