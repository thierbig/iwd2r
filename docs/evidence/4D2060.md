section, +0x02 = GetTickCount() - 30000, the port from
CChitin::GetMultiplayerGameSpyPort (0x5BE when the INI has none), the send
socket (+0x0A) and the query socket (+0x0E) created as non-blocking UDP
(FIONBIO), and the query socket bound to that port with INADDR_ANY. Any step
that fails closes what was opened and clears +0x01; +0x12 keeps the port and
+0x28 (the retry counter) is zeroed. When the open failed the original sets
+0x00 to 1 around WSACleanup and back to 0 - kept with a #design-flaw NOTE, as
the bytes have it.

open (two handles, the bound port, the master address, the tick), the
non-host refusal, the 0x5BE fallback, the inert-winsock refusal, and the close
order.
