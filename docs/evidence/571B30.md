## Notes

The function argument at `[esp+0x34]` is `std::list<LONG>&`; the local at
`[esp+0x18]` is its own `std::list<LONG>` (the sentinel is the 12-byte node
the helper allocates; `[head]` is the first node, `[node+8]` the value).
The destructor walk at 0x571C85-0x571CBD is the inlined `std::list`
destructor.

Order and filters:

1. `sub_571A60()` runs first and sets `ebx`, the level budget.
2. The budget is tested at the top of every iteration (`test ebx,ebx; jle`,
   0x571B8D); an empty list also leaves before the test.
3. A failed share is skipped with no release (0x571BC9 jumps to the advance).
4. A non-sprite is released and skipped.
5. A sprite's level is the byte at object+0x966 (`m_derivedStats.m_nLevel`,
   `GetLevel()`), zero-extended and compared signed against the budget and
   against 9. Both must hold: `level <= budget && level <= 9`.
6. The level is subtracted **before** the id is pushed onto the local list
   (0x571C1D), and the insertion is unconditionally a `push_back`: a target
   that takes the budget to exactly 0 is kept, and the next iteration's
   top-of-loop test stops the walk.
7. The target's share is then released.
8. `CProjectileArea::ApplyToTargets(local)` runs the base strike on the kept
   ids (the only virtual call into the secondary path).

The port keeps the budget test before the share (so no share is taken once
the budget is spent), the signed comparisons, the 9-level cap, the exact
spend order, and the base call. The MSVC6 list internals are replaced with
`std::list<LONG>`; its iteration order is the same input order.
