# 0x575090 CProjectileArea850384::CProjectileArea850384

An aura carrier (projectile type 283, jump-table index 282): stringless —
no cell or sound name anywhere in the ctor, so its spell identity is
unpinned by strings and the class is address-named after its vtable
0x850384. (The "type 283" mentions in docs/evidence/51EAF0.md are the
*stamped* type of case 284's Cloudkill result — the store tail's
`type − 1` — not this jump-table entry.) It follows its source, and on
its first tick and every hundredth after it re-centres on the source and
strikes every sprite newly come within 250 around, one secondary each;
it tears itself down when the source is gone or dead.

## Branch inventory

The ctor's conditionals all live inside the inlined set construction —
the `_Nil` refcount `jne` (0x575104), the temp null `je` (0x575129) and
