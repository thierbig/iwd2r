## Notes

The body is unchanged from the original; the port takes the same file-lifecycle `CCriticalSection`
as `CloseFiles` (0x7B5C30) because `CChitin::OnAltTab` (0x7914D0) calls
`CScreenConnection::EngineActivated` directly on the main thread while the AI thread's movie switch
reaches it through `CChitin::SelectEngine`, so this `Open` and the AI/main thread's `CloseFiles`
interleave. The race, its two captured stacks and the test are in `7B5C30.md` and
`tests/test_tlk_override.cpp`.
