# 0x4ED250 CtrlAltDelete_AddItemPack

A debug console "CtrlAltDelete:" cheat-command handler, free function
taking nothing (`ret`), 97 bytes (0x4ED250-0x4ED2B0). Ported as a stub in
commit 18d1aab04; unstubbed to its three real calls in commit (see
report).

## Body

Three identical call sites. Each reserves a stack slot, copy-constructs
a CString from a literal (0x7FCC88), then calls CtrlAltDelete_AddItem
(0x4ED500) — the item handout, ported in this task — with ecx restored
from esi (the dispatcher's this) and (CString by value at [esp], count,
0, 0) pushed right to left:

1. literal 0x8ABD88 "potn08", count 5;
2. literal 0x8ABD80 "potn20", count 5;
3. literal 0x8ABD78 "scrlpet", count 1.

The literals were read out of the exe image at those RVAs. The port is
the three calls exactly as the exe makes them; the use-count words past
the first are zero at every site.

## ecx / calling convention

ecx is saved into esi at entry and forwarded as the this of every
0x4ED500 call; that callee never reads it. No arguments of its own;
`ret`. Free function.

## Callers

Not in tree: the console dispatcher 0x5838A0 (deliberately unported;
docs/followups.md).
