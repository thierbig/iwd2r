     is `x / 16`, `y / 12` (`CPathSearch::GRID_SQUARE_SIZEX/Y`).
   - `GetCost(point, m_terrainTable (sprite +0x50BB), m_animation.GetPersonalSpace(),
     nTableIndex, TRUE)`; the inlined `CGameAnimation::GetPersonalSpace` carries the
     `ObjAnimation.h:2093` assertion (0x74862C-0x7486A9).
   - `m_posStart == (-1,-1)` makes the squared distance -1; an attempt is accepted when
     `m_huntingRange == 0` or the distance is at most `range * range` **and** it is attempt 1-19
     (0x7486AF-0x7486FA). Otherwise it retries or, at 20, gives up.
   - Success queues `CAIAction(MOVETOPOINT = 23, CPoint(x, y), -2, -1)` and answers ACTION_DONE
     (0x748854-0x748900). Giving up queues `CAIAction(WAIT = 63, CPoint(-1,-1), 0xFF, -1)` and
     answers ACTION_ERROR (0x748700-0x74884F).
8. Turn (nWalk 1): `AddAction(face)`, then `CAIAction(WAIT = 63, CPoint(-1,-1), rand() % 10 +
   1, -1)` (the WORD at 0x85BBAE is 10), ACTION_DONE (0x748474-0x7484E7, 0x74889D).
9. No target (render <= 14, or the AI-rate/out-of-view test): `CAIAction(WAIT = 63,
   CPoint(-1,-1), rand() % (10 * 4) + 1, -1)` and ACTION_DONE (0x748905-0x748B33).

The action ids are ACTION.IDS': 23 MoveToPoint, 63 Wait (handled by CGameAIBase::ExecuteAction),
84 Face, 85 RandomWalk, 130 RandomTurn (unlisted there; the engine's 0x82). The two unnamed
WORDs at 0x85BBA8 (2) and 0x85BBAE (10) are the random-walk timing constants.

## Oracle

No oracle: the fixture would need a live game with a loaded area, the viewport and frame rates,
the search bitmap and the sprite's action queue. The proof is the data test (see the exemption) on
the engine in-process, with `srand()` fixing the game's VC6 rand sequence: an interrupt answers
ACTION_INTERRUPTABLE with an empty queue; a frame rate of 0 queues the 1..40 Wait and the copy; out
of the view the same; in the view a RandomTurn queues Wait(1..10), Face(0..15) and the copy;
RandomWalk with an even draw queues MoveToPoint(-2) inside the area and the copy; with an odd draw
it queues the turn instead. The 20-attempt give-up is not unit-tested: with the range cap the
attempt is nearly always inside the circle, so reaching it needs 20 consecutive far draws.
