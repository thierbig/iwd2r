### Identity (survey row 25)

`CMessageHandler::AsynchronousUpdate` (0x4EE020) dispatches the CBaldurMessage
type `'m'` (MAPWORLD = 109) arm letter `'a'` (97) to 0x435E00.
The port declares `MSG_SUBTYPE_MAPWORLD_TRAVEL_ANNOUNCE` (81) and
`MSG_SUBTYPE_MAPWORLD_TRAVEL_REQUEST` (97) for the two arms. The handler now
sits in address order between its 0x4355F0 and 0x436050 neighbours.

### The payload

Exactly the layout decoded in the earlier deferral report:
`[3]` area-resref length, `[4..)` area resref bytes, the DWORD current-world
link at `4 + len1`, the entry-point length at `8 + len1`, the entry-point
string at `9 + len1`, the WORD leaving edge and the BYTE random-encounter flag
