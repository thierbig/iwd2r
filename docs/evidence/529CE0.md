## Vtable check

CProjectile's vtable 0x84D7B8 is 32 entries (+0x00..+0x7C), ending at
CallBack — exactly the virtuals our src/CProjectile.h declares. Extent
proof: the dword run at 0x84D838 (= 0x84D7B8 + 0x80) is itself a canonical
vtable head `[0x529990, 0x45B680, 0x4C7BE0, 0x536380, 0x44CB60, ...]` —
docs/evidence/536380.md records its slot-0xC entry 0x536380 as
CProjectile84E9C0::AIUpdate "also in the vtable at 0x84D838". The two
dwords past +0x7C therefore belong to the NEXT class's vtable, not to
CProjectile. CProjectileBAM genuinely extends the layout to 34 entries
(+0x00..+0x84) by introducing two new virtuals (0x5297D0 at +0x80,
0x52BD20 at +0x84), and CProjectileExploding adds Explode at +0x88 — the
appended-new-virtual pattern, which is why every BAM-family vtable reads
34+ while direct CProjectile children read 32. (This refines commit
d1de8bc4's phrasing, which called 0x5297D0/0x52BD20 "CProjectileBAM's own
overrides of CProjectile" — they are new slots BAM introduces, there being
no +0x80/+0x84 slot in CProjectile to override. No code change either
way: nothing was declared in either class for those slots.)

0x84DAD4 against 0x84D7B8, slot by slot over the first 32 entries: exactly
TWO differ (verified by reading the exe's vtable bytes).

- Slot 0x00: 0x51EA70 → 0x529E30, the scalar deleting dtor (`push esi; mov
  esi,ecx; call 0x529E50; test [esp+8],1; je; push esi; call 0x7FC984;
  mov eax,esi; pop esi; ret 0x4`). Implicit ours — the class has no
  member beyond CProjectile's.
- Slot 0x1B (Fire, +0x6C): 0x78E740 → 0x5818F0. Not in tree (no port, no
  fork body); left undeclared per the IcewindCProjectileScorcher
  precedent — a declared-but-undefined virtual would become the key
  function and leave the vtable unemitted. Ours inherits the base
  CProjectile::Fire in that slot (the same accepted structural deviation
  as ctors 0x52E230, 0x5806C0, 0x52F260, 0x52FC20 and 0x52FE90).

Vtable extent: 32 entries (+0x00..+0x7C). The dwords at +0x80/+0x84
(0x52CBA0, 0x45B680) are the head of the NEXT vtable in .rdata,
0x84DB54 = [0x52CBA0, 0x45B680, 0x4C7BE0, 0x52B900, 0x44CB60, ...] — a
BAM-family class (slot 3 = 0x52B900, CProjectileBAM's parked AIUpdate),
not overrides of this class. The same boundary effect explains why
diffing the sibling CProjectile84D8B8 (0x84D8B8, also 32 entries, diffs =
dtor 0x5308E0 + Fire 0x529950 only — the calibrated Fire-only shape)
against CProjectile shows apparent extra diffs past +0x7C: the vtables
that FOLLOW them in .rdata belong to different classes. RTTI is stripped
in this exe, so extents are inferred from the canonical-vtable-head
boundary shape, as with 0x84DE0C and 0x84DE94.
