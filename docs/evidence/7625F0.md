# 0x7625F0 FUN_007625f0

The XEquipItem helper of CGameAIBase::ExecuteAction case 294 (the only
caller, 0x451D73). Ported as `SHORT CGameSprite::XEquipItem(CString sName,
LONG nSlot, LONG nEquip)` in src/CGameSprite.cpp; the owner is CGameSprite,
not CGameAIBase: the body indexes `m_equipment` (+0x4AD8, 51 `CItem*`) off
`this` and calls FindItemPersonal, CItem::Equip/Unequip and
CGameAIBase::PutItemGround on it. Ghidra files the export under
CGameSprite.cpp.

## Signature and frame

`__thiscall`, `ret 0xc`: `ecx` is the target sprite (the call site hands it
the action's target share, ebp at 0x451D71). Three 4-byte stack arguments:
arg1 a CString passed **by value** -- the callee destroys it at the epilogue
(`lea ecx,[esp+0x1C]; call CString_Destruct 0x7FCC1A` at 0x762716) -- then
arg2 and arg3. The call site (0x451D4A-0x451D73) builds them as
`(m_curAction.GetString1(), GetSpecifics(), GetSpecifics2())`: GetSpecifics2
is pushed first (arg3), GetSpecifics next (arg2), and GetString1 writes the
CString into the arg1 slot through its sret push.

No RNG, no stat or formula read. The globals: 0x8485C4 ACTION_DONE,
0x8485CA ACTION_ERROR, and the WORD at 0x855128 = 24
(CScreenInventory::PERSONAL_INVENTORY_SIZE), the inventory scan bound.

## Steps (0x7625F0-0x76273B)

1. `FindItemPersonal(sName, 0, FALSE)` (0x71D310; pushes 0, 0, &arg1 at
   0x76260A-0x76261B). `ax == 0xFFFF` returns ACTION_ERROR through the
   epilogue (0x762620-0x76262D).
2. arg3 (`[esp+0x24]`, 0x762632) non-zero is the equip mode:
   - arg2 (`[esp+0x20]`) is the destination slot, kept in edi; the source
     index is the SHORT result sign-extended (`movsx ecx, ax`), the array
     base `lea ebx,[esi+ecx*4+0x4AD8]`.
   - `m_items[nFound]->Equip(this, nSlot, FALSE)` (0x4E8860, 0x76264E).
   - when `m_items[nSlot] != NULL` (full dword index at 0x762653), that item
     is unequipped first: `m_items[nSlot]->Unequip(this, nSlot, TRUE, FALSE)`
     (0x4E8DF0, 0x762664).
   - the two pointers swap: `m_items[nFound]` and `m_items[nSlot]`
     (0x762669-0x762679). NOTE: this second index is the destination slot's
     low word sign-extended (`movsx edx, di`, 0x76266B) while the occupied
     test and the two calls use the full dword; identical for every slot the
     action carries and kept verbatim.
3. arg3 zero is the unequip mode:
   - `m_items[nFound]->Unequip(this, nFound, TRUE, FALSE)` (0x762690).
   - `FindItemPersonal(sName, 0, TRUE)` (0x71D310; pushes 1, 0, &arg1 at
     0x762695-0x7626A0). Its SHORT result is edi; -1 returns ACTION_DONE
     through 0x76270F, so an item that was already in the inventory (slots
     18-50) stays where it is.
   - first blank scan: `for (nBlank = 0; nBlank < 24 && m_items[18+nBlank] !=
     NULL; nBlank++)` (0x7626AD-0x7626CD), the array base `[esi+0x4B20]` =
     `m_items[18]`.
   - blank found: `m_items[18+nBlank] = m_items[nEquiped]` (0x7626DB).
   - no blank: `PutItemGround(m_items[nEquiped])` (0x463310, 0x7626FC).
   - `m_items[nEquiped] = NULL` (0x762704) either way.
4. Both modes return ACTION_DONE (the WORD at 0x8485C4, 0x76270F/0x762716).

## Tests

`tests/test_ai.cpp`, "ai: ExecuteAction 294 XEquipItem equips with a swap and
unequips to the pack" ([ai][data][ar3000]), drives action 294 through
`CGameAIBase::ExecuteAction` on a CHARBASE sprite target (actee =
CAIObjectType with the sprite's id) in AR3000. The inputs the body and its
callees read are pinned: the target is a sprite whose immunity list does not
hold the caller's AI type (GetTargetShareType's rejection) and which has an
animation, the caller is no sprite (the blind test is not taken), and every
item resref is a type 34 item (CItem::sub_4E9250's no-op arm) with no
equipped effects (Unequip's effect-list/world-timer work stays out). Cases:

- missing item: ACTION_ERROR, no slot changes (step 1 miss);
- equip into a blank slot: pointer identity, source slot cleared (step 2
  without the Unequip);
- equip into an occupied slot: the newcomer takes the destination and the
  old occupant the source's slot, exactly the pointer swap, and the old
  occupant's Unequip ran (step 2 with it);
- unequip from an equipment slot: item at slot 5 lands in slot 18 (first
  blank) by pointer identity (step 3);
- the same with slot 18 occupied: it lands in slot 19, slot 18 untouched
  (step 3's scan);
- unequip of an item already in the inventory (slot 20): it stays there
  (step 3's second lookup miss);
- full pack: the item goes to the ground pile through PutItemGround (the
  action's ACTION_DONE), the equipment slot is cleared, the original pointer
  is in CInfGame::m_lDisposableItems, and after AsynchronousUpdate the pile's
  copy carries the resref and usage count.

Not exercised: the out-of-range destination index above (an action never
carries one; the two spellings agree for every legal slot), and CItem's own
equipped-effect removal inside Unequip (gems carry no equipped effects; that
is 4E8DF0.md's row).
