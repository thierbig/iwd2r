## Notes

The budget is the immediate `0xc8` = 200 in `ebp` (0x57400C), before the
walk. Per target:

- Failed share skipped; non-sprite released and skipped.
- The hit points are the signed 16-bit word at object+0x5C0: `CGameSprite::
  m_baseStats.m_hitPoints` (`m_baseStats` is at 0x5A4; `m_hitPoints` is its
  0x1C member).
- Two guards, in this order: `cmp ax, 0x64; jg` rejects **more than 100**
  hit points (signed 16-bit compare), then `movsx eax, ax; cmp eax, ebp;
  jg` rejects more than the remaining budget (signed 32-bit compare). So hit
  points of exactly 100 pass the cap and negative hit points qualify.
- On both guards passing, the budget is reduced by the target's hit points
  (0x574083, immediately before the insertion) and the id is appended to the
  kept list. The walk does not stop at 0: later targets are still examined,
  and one whose hit points fit the (possibly zero or negative) remainder is
  still kept.
- Every shared target is released; the kept list goes to
  `CProjectileArea::ApplyToTargets` (call at 0x5740E4).
