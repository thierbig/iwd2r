## Notes

The host's weather broadcast. It needs the session open (`GetSessionOpen`,
0x8CF6D8 + 0x1032) **and** owned (`GetSessionHosting`, +0x1033), else FALSE;
a failed 2-byte `CreateBuffer` also returns FALSE. The payload is the
`wWeatherFlags` WORD alone (2 bytes, no padding), sent to the empty player
name (all players) with `SEND_ALL_PLAYERS | SEND_GUARANTEED` (0x100 | 0x200
from 0x85E660 | 0x85E664). The wire is type 87 `MSG_TYPE_WEATHER` (the byte at
0x84CFD1) and subtype 119 `MSG_SUBTYPE_WEATHER_BROADCAST` (0x84CFD2); both
constants are added here (WSO has no names, T122 §3). The decompile keeps the
send's result unused and returns 1 on the same path.

**Return type fixed:** the exe returns in AL (`mov al,1` at 0x437022,
`xor al,al` at 0x43703A) with `ret 4`, so the declaration is changed from
`void` to `BOOLEAN` - the tree (and WSO's header) had `void` because his body
is a stub. The three `CWeather` call sites ignore the value, so nothing else
changes; the neighbouring `SendReformPartyAnnounceStatus` is `BOOLEAN` for the
same reason (its exe body also ends `mov al,1`/`xor al,al`).

Wire bytes under `MINIMAL_PACKET_SIZE`: `[0] = 0xFF`, `[1] = 87`, `[2] = 119`,
`[3..4] = the flags WORD` little endian (5 bytes). `tests/test_network.cpp`
("msg: the weather broadcast writes WEATHER/119 for the host only",
`[network][msg]`) selects `SERV_PROV_TCP_IP` and pins the closed-session and
client guards (buffer untouched), the host's flags/type/subtype and the
payload for 0x1234 and 0. Against the stub, 6 of its 12 assertions fail.

The decompile was drafted with Ghidra 12.1.4 from the labelled project on the
box (`tools/oracle.py evidence 0x436F50`).
