## Notes

1. The guard is the session only (there is no hosting check unlike the demand senders):
   `if (!g_pChitin->cNetwork.GetSessionOpen()) return FALSE;`.
2. `hasCharacterInfo != TRUE` short-circuits to the destructors and TRUE.
3. The port covers the whole flow above; two spots are marked with `// TODO` and NOTEs
   because their exact argument plumbing could not be read confidently without the Ghidra draft:
   the world-screen portrait-control test at 0x42E9C5 (the port always calls EnablePortrait) and
   the old-area placement helper 0x6F3B80 (seven arguments). The rest is written against the
   repo's helpers.
4. Follow-up when the box returns: the Ghidra decompile draft (`tools/oracle.py evidence
   0x42E630`) to confirm the two NOTEd blocks and the oracle case; the character marshal needs a
   real sprite, so the current proof is the guards.
