# 0x5AE020 CInfGame::ApplyPartyRoster

`BOOL CInfGame::ApplyPartyRoster(LONG* pDesiredPartyIds, SHORT
nDesiredOverflowCount, LONG* pDesiredOverflowIds)` — reconciles the live
party against a desired roster: trims the overflow bench of ids the
caller no longer wants, benches newly desired overflow ids, then walks
the six active slots, removing sitting members whose slot wants someone
else and adding the wanted newcomers. Returns FALSE on the two early
rejects (a bench sprite that cannot be denied, an add that fails), TRUE
otherwise. Member of CInfGame, declared at src/CInfGame.h:264, defined
at src/CInfGame.cpp:12813-13185. No Ghidra pseudo-code embed: written
and verified against the instruction-level disassembly (the decompiler
