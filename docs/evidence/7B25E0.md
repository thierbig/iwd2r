# 0x7B25E0 CVidTile::BltDualTile8To16

## What it is

The 16-bpp target of the dual-tile Render dispatcher 0x7B1F70's bpp switch
(`case 16`, jump at 0x7B2262). Thiscall on the CVidTile, `ret 0x24` = nine
stack args: `BltDualTile8To16(WORD* pSurface, LONG lPitch, BYTE* pData,
BYTE* pDualData, CResTile* pResDualTile, BYTE nDitherSeed, const CSize&
blitSize, LONG nDataJump, DWORD dwFlags)`. It blends this tile's palette
with the dual tile's palette per pixel through the 64x64x32 blend cube,
dithered by the seed's phase. 1,120 bytes (0x7B25E0-0x7B2A3F).

## Port

`src/CVidImage.cpp` `CVidTile::BltDualTile8To16`, declaration in
`src/CVidImage.h` after BltStencilTile8To16; the new static
`CVidImage::rgbDualTempPal` (0x9060F4) beside rgbTempPal in the cpp and
header. The blend table is ensured through BuildBlendTable() (guarded
helper) instead of the exe's inlined copy; the NULL-guarded video-mode
reads, the post-Realize height check and the WORD truncation of the
reassembled pixel are faithful.

## Callers

The dual-tile Render 0x7B1F70's `case 16` (0x7B2262) - ported in this
task's sibling commit; sole ultimate caller CInfTileSet::RenderToPrimary
0x5CB260 default-tile arm. The 24-bpp (0x7CE3D0) and 32-bpp (0x7CE930)
siblings of this blitter are ported and wired into the dispatcher's other
two cases.
