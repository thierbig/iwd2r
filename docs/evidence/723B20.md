move/load paths call it before queueing a `CMessageSpriteUpdate`, so the next
marshal is a full update against a clean record. Ported now; the earlier stub

**Not cleared (and deliberately so):** the 64-byte `m_baseSkills` (+0x76), the
64-byte `m_skills` (+0xB6) and `field_106` (+0xF6). The exe's stores stop at
+0x74 and jump straight to the flag at +0xF8; a full update (the flag this
function sets) sends and refreshes those three from the sprite afterwards. The
follow-up brief's summary list said those offsets are cleared; the listing
does not, and the port follows the listing. The port's `std::bitset` storage
is wider than the exe's for `m_visualEffects` (8 vs 4 bytes); `.reset()` clears
all bits, which is the same observable state.

**Free semantics:** the exe's `operator_delete` frees its `operator_new`
path/portrait buffers (the encoder's counterpart). Our ported encoder
allocates those shadows with `new LONG[...]` (src/CMessage.cpp:20072/20102) and
frees them with `delete[]`, so `Initialize` and the destructor use `delete[]`
to match; noted in the body.
