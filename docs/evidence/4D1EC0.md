## Notes

Sends one GameSpy master heartbeat from the send socket (+0x0A) to the master
address (+0x06) at port htons(0x6CFC), holding the reply section
(CNetwork::field_F6A, chitin+0x18BC) across it. The query is

    +0x01 == 1: `\heartbeat\<port>\gamename\<name>\statechanged\` + LF
    otherwise:  `\heartbeat\<port>\gamename\<name>\` + LF

with `<port>` the GameSpy port at +0x12 and `<name>` from
CChitin::GetGameSpyGameName. a1 is compared against 1 exactly. The datagram is
a copy of the sprintf result with **no terminator** (the original allocates and
copies strlen bytes and passes strlen to sendto), which the test pins
byte-for-byte for both forms.

It does nothing when +0x01 is clear or the session is not the host
(g_pChitin->cNetwork.m_bIsHost, chitin+0x1033).

**Divergence, NOTE in the code:** the original's two allocation-failure paths
return without LeaveCriticalSection (#design-flaw); the port leaves it, as in
0x4D0980.

No oracle: the master servers are gone and the platform's socket calls are
inert. tests/test_gamespy.cpp checks both strings, the destination port and
address, the flags, and the two early-outs, through the fake sendto.
