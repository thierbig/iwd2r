## Notes

The budget is the immediate `0x96` = 150 in `ebp` (0x5779EC), before the
walk. Per target:

- Failed share skipped; non-sprite released and skipped.
- The hit points are the signed 16-bit word at object+0x5C0
  (`m_baseStats.m_hitPoints`, the same field Power Word Kill reads).
- One guard: `movsx eax, word` then `cmp eax, ebp; jg` rejects more than the
  remaining budget. There is no 100-point cap here, unlike Power Word Kill.
  Hit points of exactly 150 pass; negative hit points qualify.
- On passing, the budget is reduced by the target's hit points (0x577A5A,
  before the node is linked) and the id is appended. The walk does not stop
  when the budget reaches 0; later targets are still examined and can still
  fit a zero or negative remainder.
- Every shared target is released; the kept list goes to
  `CProjectileArea::ApplyToTargets` (0x577ABB).
