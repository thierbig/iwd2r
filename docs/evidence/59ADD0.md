## Notes

0x59ADD0-0x59AEED. The destructor inlines `Clean()`: it walks the buckets
downwards (0x59ADF2-0x59AE41), frees each chain through `operator_delete`
with `pNext` cleared first (0x59AE1B-0x59AE2E), and then clears the bucket
head (`mov [ecx + ebp], ebx` at 0x59AE36) before moving on. It then frees the
control-change list under the critical section.

The ported `Clean()` (src/CGameRemoteObjectArray.cpp, inlined into this
destructor and called from `CGameObjectArray::Clean` 0x59A9D0) freed the
chains but never cleared the bucket heads, so a second `Clean()` walked the
freed nodes again: a double free. This was invisible while the ported
`Add` dropped the first entry of every empty bucket (see 59AEF0.md); once
that fix made entries survive, the engine shutdown in
tests/test_text_overlay.cpp aborted with "double free or corruption" and
tests/test_gam.cpp's save/load test failed. The fix adds the original's
bucket-head clear after each chain, matching 0x59AE36.

Reached only at shutdown/`CGameObjectArray::Clean` (0x59A9D0); there is no
standalone oracle/emu case for a destructor and the differential emulator
needs our MSVC x86 build and venv, which this box does not have.
