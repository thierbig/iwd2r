# 0x45D560 CGameAIBase::GetReaction

`BYTE CGameAIBase::GetReaction(CGameSprite* pSprite)`, 318 bytes
(0x45D560-0x45D69E), ported whole in daf67713 (`// 0x45D560` in
src/CGameAIBase.cpp) as the Reaction trio's shared callee. Its callers
are the three Reaction arms of EvaluateStatusTrigger (0x455500,
0x455573, 0x4555E8), one per arm of the trio spelled thrice.

## What it does

The sprite's enemy-ally byte picks the reputation band: EA_PC reads the
game's reputation / 10 (CInfGame m_nReputation, word at +0x387A, idiv by
the word 10 at 0x851896), anyone else the sprite's own derived
m_nReputation (word at +0x994). The reaction byte is this AI object's
reaction roll (m_reactionRoll +0x590) plus the charisma column of the
game's rule tables (m_tReactionModCharisma, the sprite's derived m_nCHR
at +0x97E minus one) plus the reputation column
(m_tReactionModReputation, band minus one), truncated to a byte. A
ranger caller (GetObjectType TYPE_SPRITE and the class mask bit at
+0x18) whose race the sprite's hates (IsHatedRace 0x546800 over the
sprite's AI type and base file header) has four points taken off,
floored at zero.

## The inlined rule-table accessor

Both table reads are C2DArray::GetAtLong (0x403440) inlined: the listing
spells the accessor's bound checks (rows word +0x20, columns word +0x22
of each table, negative index test), the data pointer at +0x18, the
default cell at +0x1C and the cell dereference around each of the two
calls to 0x7E7B03 (0x45D5EB charisma, 0x45D62B reputation) -- the parse
core behind the CRT atol entry. The port keeps the accessor as the
in-tree C2DArray::GetAtLong, which performs the same checks, default
fallback and iwd2r_atol conversion; the parity waiver above records the
one-callee spelling difference this inlining causes.
